What is Commit2Act?

Commit2Act is a platform encouraging people to take meaningful steps to reduce the impact of fast fashion by making sustainable choices.

How can I participate?

You can take actions like buying second-hand, repairing clothes, supporting sustainable brands, and logging your impact through our tools.

Is there a cost to join

No, Commit2Act is free and open to anyone interested in creating a more sustainable future.

Where can I learn more?

We share helpful content on our site and social media. More resources will be added soon.
